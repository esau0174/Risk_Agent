# Potential Future Exposure

## Purpose
Summarize a percentile-based counterparty exposure profile across future time horizons.

## Inputs
- Netting set identifiers
- Time in years
- 95% PFE values
- Optional 99% PFE values

## Calculation
Select the maximum PFE value across the supplied profile and report the time at which that peak occurs.

## Outputs
- Peak PFE 95 and time of peak
- Optional peak PFE 99 and time of peak

## Assumptions
- The supplied exposure quantiles and time grid are internally consistent.

## Limitations
- PFE is not expected exposure or a credit loss estimate.
- The supplied or toy-mode profile is not generated by a full Monte Carlo pricing engine here.

## Validation Rules
- PFE values and time points must be non-negative.
- PFE 99 must be greater than or equal to PFE 95 when both are present.
- Commentary peak values and times must match deterministic metrics within tolerance.
