# Counterparty Exposure Limitations

## Purpose
Define interpretation boundaries for the counterparty exposure and PFE workflow.

## Inputs
- Supplied exposure profile
- Calculated PFE and expected exposure metrics

## Calculation
No numerical calculation is performed. The note supplies counterparty-specific disclosure context.

## Outputs
- Counterparty model assumptions and limitations
- Scope exclusions for generated commentary

## Assumptions
- The input profile has already been validated for schema and non-negative exposures.

## Limitations
- The profile is supplied or toy-mode, not generated by a full Monte Carlo pricing engine.
- Market simulation, collateral, legal netting, trade valuation, and wrong-way risk are not modeled.
- XVA, PD, LGD, and EAD are outside the current scope.

## Validation Rules
- Commentary must disclose the supplied or toy-mode profile limitation.
- Commentary must not claim production-grade Monte Carlo or XVA analytics.
- Methodology references must match retrieved counterparty notes.
