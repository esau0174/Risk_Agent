# Concentration Risk

Concentration risk describes exposure that is unusually large in a single holding, asset class,
sector, style, or risk factor. A portfolio can be concentrated even when no single position is
dominant if several holdings share similar exposures.

Ticker-level composition can suggest overlapping growth, technology, semiconductor, AI, broad
equity, or rate-sensitive bond exposures. These observations are qualitative unless a formal
sector, holdings, or factor model is calculated.
